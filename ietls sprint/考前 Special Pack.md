15. 旅館入住率在暑假期間大幅上升，但在冬季明顯下降，波動相當明顯。

# 📈 3. Task 1 動詞搭配 20 題

**任務：**  
每題寫 **1 句 Task 1 句子**，必須用指定動詞模板（我在題目後面標出）。

---

1. Internet usage: 20% → 75%（2000–2020）  
    → 用：**increased from A to B**
    
2. Car ownership: 60% → 45%（10 年間）  
    → 用：**fell from A to B**
    
3. Tablet ownership: 10% → 35%（上升 25 個百分點）  
    → 用：**increased by X**
    
4. Smartphone usage: 90%（最高點，在 2018 年）  
    → 用：**peaked at**
    
5. Unemployment: 3%（最低點，在 2015 年）  
    → 用：**reached a low of**
    
6. Oil production: 50m → 30m（2000–2010）  
    → 用：**dropped from A to B** 或 **dropped by X**
    
7. Bus usage: 40% → 40%（整段期間幾乎沒變）  
    → 用：**remained stable at around**
    
8. Tourist numbers: 2m → 4m → 2m（先上升再回到原點）  
    → 用：**rose to… then fell back to…**
    
9. Electricity consumption: 200 → 260 kWh per household  
    → 用：**increased gradually from A to B**
    
10. Coal use: 80m → 20m（大幅下降）  
    → 用：**fell sharply from A to B**
    
11. Student enrolment: 15,000 → 22,000（8 年間穩定上升）  
    → 用：**grew steadily from A to B**
    
12. DVD rentals: 40m → 5m（十年間）  
    → 用：**declined dramatically from A to B**
    
13. Online sales: 5% → 35%（上升 30 個百分點）  
    → 用：**increased by X**
    
14. Elderly population: 8% → 18%  
    → 用：**almost doubled from A to B**
    
15. Birth rate: 2.1 → 1.3（下降）  
    → 用：**fell to a low of**
    
16. Train usage: 25% → 30% → 28%（小幅波動）  
    → 用：**fluctuated around X before ending at Y**
    
17. Average income: $20k → $35k  
    → 用：**rose significantly from A to B**
    
18. Recycling rate: 10% → 40%  
    → 用：**increased fourfold from A to B**
    
19. Sugar consumption in snacks: 30% → 20%  
    → 用：**decreased by X**
    
20. Three categories都上升，其中 A: 10%→50%，B: 20%→40%，C: 15%→25%。  
    → 寫 1 句 overview，必須提 **A 增幅最大**（可用 grew the most / showed the most significant growth 等）

# ✍️ 5. Task 2 Intro 練習 5 題（套用你五種模板）

**任務：**  
每題只寫 **Introduction 一段**，使用你老師給的 5 種 pattern（Type 1–5 自由搭配）。

---

1. **Opinion 題（Type 1 / 2 推薦）**  
    _Some people believe that working from home is more beneficial for employees, while others think that working in an office is better for productivity. To what extent do you agree or disagree?_
    
2. **Discuss Both Views 題（Type 2 推薦）**  
    _Some people think that it is better for children to grow up in the countryside, while others believe that cities offer more advantages for a child’s development. Discuss both views and give your opinion._
    
3. **Advantages / Disadvantages 題（Type 3B 推薦）**  
    _In many countries, online shopping is replacing traditional shops. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of this development._
    
4. **Cause & Effect 題（Type 4 推薦）**  
    _In many cities, people are becoming increasingly dependent on cars. Why is this happening, and what effects does this have on individuals and society?_
    
5. **Problem & Solution 題（Type 5 推薦）**  
    _Many university students feel stressed about their studies and future careers. What problems does this cause, and what can universities do to help them?_
