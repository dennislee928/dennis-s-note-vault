## 一、教育 Education（+ 政府）

### 教育 Skeleton Set

#### EDU-B1（好處面：機會與公平）

1. **One major benefit of [education policy] is that it can improve students’ long-term opportunities.**
    
2. When schools provide [specific support/resources], students from different backgrounds can develop similar academic and practical skills.
    
3. This makes it easier for them to enter higher education or the job market, rather than being limited by their family’s income or social class.
    

> `[education policy]` 例：making university free / offering more scholarships / providing online courses in rural areas  
> `[specific support/resources]` 例：extra tutoring, free textbooks, high-speed internet

—

#### EDU-B2（壞處面：壓力與心理健康）

1. **However, a serious drawback of [education policy] is that it may increase pressure on students.**
    
2. Many young people already face heavy workloads and constant testing, so adding [this requirement] can leave them exhausted and unmotivated.
    
3. In the long run, this stress may harm their mental health and even reduce their interest in learning.
    

> `[this requirement]` 例：compulsory community service / extra evening classes / more high-stakes exams

---

## 二、科技 Technology（+ 媒體）

### 科技 Skeleton Set

#### TECH-B1（好處面：效率與生產力）

1. **One clear advantage of [technology development] is that it makes everyday tasks faster and more convenient.**
    
2. For instance, people can now complete [task] in a few clicks instead of spending hours in person or on paper.
    
3. This allows individuals and businesses to save time and focus on more creative or strategic work.
    

> `[technology development]` 例：online banking / workplace automation / AI tools  
> `[task]` 例：paying bills, booking tickets, processing documents

—

#### TECH-B2（壞處面：社交與心理問題）

1. **On the other hand, a key disadvantage of relying on [technology] is that it can reduce face-to-face interaction.**
    
2. People may spend long hours on screens, chatting online instead of building deep relationships in real life.
    
3. As a result, feelings of isolation and miscommunication can increase, especially among young users.
    

> `[technology]` 例：social media platforms / smartphones / online gaming

## 三、健康 Health（+ 政府 / 媒體）

### 健康 Skeleton Set

#### HEALTH-B1（個人責任：生活習慣）

1. **One important way to improve public health is to encourage individuals to take more responsibility for their lifestyle choices.**
    
2. When people are informed about the risks of poor diet or lack of exercise, they are more likely to change their daily habits.
    
3. Over time, these small personal decisions can lower the rates of obesity, heart disease, and other chronic illnesses.
    

—

#### HEALTH-B2（政府＋媒體：規管與宣導）

1. **At the same time, governments and the media also play a crucial role in shaping people’s health behaviours.**
    
2. If authorities invest in public campaigns and restrict misleading advertisements, citizens receive clearer messages about what is truly safe or harmful.
    
3. This combination of regulation and information can create a healthier environment, rather than leaving people to make decisions alone.

## 四、跨主題組合：教育 × 科技 / 政府 × 媒體（萬用）

這兩組專門給「教育＋科技」或「政府＋媒體」混合題用：

#### COMBO-B1（教育 × 科技：正面）

1. **Integrating technology into education can make lessons more engaging and personalised.**
    
2. Digital tools allow teachers to adapt materials to different levels, so weaker students receive extra support while stronger students are challenged.
    
3. This targeted approach often leads to better learning outcomes than a traditional one-size-fits-all classroom.
    

—

#### COMBO-B2（政府 × 媒體 × 科技：需要管制）

1. **However, without strong government rules, media and technology companies may put profit before the public interest.**
    
2. They might promote unhealthy products or spread sensational information simply to attract more views and clicks.
    
3. In this situation, both physical health and social trust can be damaged, which is why stricter oversight is often necessary.

## 三、健康 Health（個人 / 政府 / 媒體）

|角度|核心論點標籤|適用題目關鍵字|快速例子情境|
|---|---|---|---|

|好處|個人責任、生活習慣|personal responsibility, obesity|個人選擇多走路、少喝含糖飲料，體重與血壓下降|
|好處|公共醫療投資|healthcare spending, public hospitals|政府擴建公立醫院與培養醫護人員，縮短看診等待時間|
|好處|科技輔助醫療|telemedicine, health apps|偏鄉患者透過視訊看診，使用 APP 追蹤血壓與血糖|
|好處|健康教育與媒體宣導|health campaigns, awareness|電視與社群廣告提醒吸菸風險，吸菸率逐年下降|
|壞處|醫療資源分配不均|rural healthcare, inequality|都市集中了專科醫院，偏鄉居民看病需長途奔波|
|壞處|現代壓力與心理疾病|stress, mental health|白領上班族加班與績效壓力導致憂鬱與焦慮症|
|壞處|不健康產品行銷|junk food ads, alcohol, tobacco|速食與含糖飲料廣告鎖定兒童，影響飲食習慣|
|壞處|醫療商業化|private hospitals, profit|私立醫院收費昂貴，低收入者無法負擔高品質治療|
|好處|政府管制改善環境健康|pollution control, public policy|政府限制工廠排放與塑膠袋使用，改善空氣與環境品質|
