## Part 1 — Type 3A：Compare & Contrast（比較題）6 題

通用模板（給你套用，不用每次一模一樣）：

> It has long been debated whether **A** or **B** is more effective/beneficial.  
> Although both options offer clear advantages, their differences cannot be ignored.  
> The main benefits of each approach will be compared in the following paragraphs, and I will also explain **my own view**.

寫 intro 時務必做到：

- 有提到 **兩個選項 A / B**
    
- 有說明「兩邊都有優點＋會比較」
    
- 可以加一句簡短 opinion（如 I believe A is generally more suitable for…）

## 🟢 Part 3 — Type 5：Problem & Solution（問題＋解決）6 題

通用模板：

> **[問題主題]** has become a serious concern in many countries.  
> This issue leads to several significant problems for **individuals / communities / the environment**,  
> but there are also a number of measures that could be taken to address it.  
> Both the main problems and possible solutions will be discussed in the following paragraphs.

關鍵是：

- 第一句：直接點名「這是嚴重問題」
    
- 第二句：提到 **problems**
    
- 第三句：提到 **measures / solutions**
    
- 不要寫成「reasons」題
