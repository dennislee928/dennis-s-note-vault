## 🔹Part A — 修正 which + 不可數 + 動詞搭配（6 題）

任務說明：  
每題都有一個「**有問題的英文句子**」，請你：

1. 保留原本的意思（不要改內容邏輯）
    
2. 修成 **1 句自然英文**
    
3. 一定要保留：
    
    - 不可數名詞（information / research / advice / progress / traffic / pollution / equipment / data）
        
    - `, which + 結果`（合法 which，主語不換）
        

---

### A-1（AI ＋ 工作壓力）

Many information about artificial intelligence at work was published by the government, which made people  know their jobs will be replaced soon.
(information不可數+動詞錯誤)
(made + S + v)


> 任務：
> 
> - information（不可數）
>     
> - which 後面用：has helped / has made workers more aware / has reduced unnecessary fear
>

### A-2（心理健康 ＋ 研究）

Recent researches on social media and mental health has positive results, which shows people should use phones less at night.

Recent research on social media and mental health has positive results, which shows that people should use phones less at night.
(research不可數)
(fragment, 少that)




> 任務：
> 
> - research on …（不可數）
>     
> - which 後面用：shows / indicates / suggests that…
>     
> - 注意：research + 單複數 + has/have
>

### A-3（育兒建議）

Experts gave many advices on how parents can guide children’s Internet use, which helps them to set suitable rules in the family.


Experts gave much advice on how parents can guide children’s Internet use, which helps them set suitable rules in their family.








> 任務：
> 
> - advice on …（不可數）
>     
> - which has helped parents… / which makes it easier for parents to…
>     
> - 避免：advices, helps them to set（可以改成 helps them set）
>

## 🔹Part B — 自造 which + 不可數 結果句（新情境 6 題）

任務說明：

- 每題用 **1 句英文** 完成
    
- 主語必須是：**information / recent research / advice / progress / traffic / equipment / data** 之一
    
- 句型必須長這樣：
    

> 主句：  
> **S (不可數名詞) + V …**,  
> **which + has Vpp / V-s + 結果**

例如（示意結構，不要照抄）：

- Recent research on online learning **has shown that…**, which **has helped schools design better courses**.
    

---

### B-1（健康飲食資訊）

政府提供了大量關於健康飲食的資訊，這讓更多人開始檢查自己的飲食習慣。

Much information about healthy eating is provided by the govrenments, which makes more people start to inspect their own eating habits.




> 限制：
> 
> - 主語：Information about healthy eating
>     
> - 結果：which has encouraged more people to review / change their eating habits
>

Recent research on efficiency of remote working indicates that it's easier for emplyees to matain balance between work and life if they can work from home.

> - 主語：Recent research on remote work / Recent research on working from home
