# # **句界（Sentence Boundary）**

### **定義：**

每個句子必須：

- 明確有主詞
    
- 明確有動詞
    
- 明確有句號結尾
    
- 標點＋空格格式正確
    
- 不可以用逗號硬接句子
    
- 不可以用 which 引述新動作
    

考官最討厭的句界錯誤是：

- 句子太長（35–45 字都塞在一起）
    
- which + 新動作,- Which 子句只能接在前一句後面, 放句首 → fragment（不完整句）
    
- though/although 用錯位
    
- therefore / however / moreover 用法錯

### **定義：**

句號、逗號、分號、冒號後面**一定要**加空格。  
例如：
## **1A. 逗號連兩句（Comma Splice / Run-on）**

**錯誤模式：**

- A, B （兩個完整子句被逗號亂接）
    

**你的原句：**

- “Bus usage decreased by 10%, which could cause…”（which 下面又接新動作）
    
- “The harbour became clearer, the hotel was expanded.”
    
- “The number fluctuated, followed by…”（結構錯）
    

**Task 2 也有大量：**

- “Some people think A, others argue B.”
    

**正確修法：**

- A. B.（分兩句）
    
- A; however, B.
    
- A, while B.

## **1B. Fragment（不完整子句）**

**典型錯法：**

- “In addition, which …”
    
- “Followed by the percentage of …”
    
- “Which shows that …” 開頭句
    
- “Making cities crowded.”（缺主詞）

# **SECTION 2 — Task 1 動詞搭配錯誤（Templates Misuse）**

### → **你的 Task 1 最大扣分來源**

---

## **2A. peak to（錯 100%）→ peak at（對）**

你的錯例：

- “peaked to 90%”
    The percentage peaked at 90% in 2000. Following that, it experienced a slight decrease.
    The percentage peaked at 90% in 2000, followed by a slight decrease.
- “peaked to 97% eventually”
    After that, the number peaked at 97% eventually and remained unchanged.
- “peaked eventually to …”
    Eventually, the number peaked  at 97%.

---

## **2B. increased largely（錯）**

正確：

- increased significantly
    The number increased significantly, rising from 25% in 2000 to 48% in 2010, almost doubled.
- rose sharply
     The number rose substantially, climbing from  25% to 48% over the same period, almost doubled.

---

## **2C. dropped slightly by 40%（矛盾）**

slightly ≠ 40%  
這會被 examiner 認為是 **邏輯錯誤**。

---

## **2D. fluctuated but increased finally（錯）**

fluctuate = 起伏，不帶方向  
後面不能直接接 a final increase。  
要寫成：

- fluctuated around X before rising to Y
    The percentage fluctuated around 58% to 72% between 2000 and 2007 before it rose steadily to 90% in 2010. After that , it peaked at 98% in 2012 eventually.
