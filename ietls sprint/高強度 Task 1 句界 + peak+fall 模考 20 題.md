## **A. peaked at（6 題）**

### **1.**

Smartphone usage reached 94% in 2021 （最高點）。  
→ 用 **peaked at** 造句。

Smartphone usage peaked at 94% in 2021.

### **2.**

Tourism revenue hit its maximum, 130 billion dollars, in 2017.  
→ 用 peaked at。

Tourism revenue peaked at 130 billion dollars in 2017.
### **3.**

Car ownership reached its highest level of 82% in 2015.  
→ 用 peaked at。
Car ownership peaked at 82% in 2015.  
### **4.**

Internet access reached 99% in 2023.  
→ 用 peaked at。
Internet access peaked at 99% in 2023.  
### **5.**

Steel production hit its peak of 70 million tonnes in 2008.  
→ 用 peaked at。

Steel production peaked at 70 million tonnes in 2008.  
### **6.**

Daily water consumption reached 360 liters per person in 2005.  
→ 用 peaked at。
Daily water consumption peaked at 360 liters per person in 2005.

## **B. fell from A to B（7 題）**

### **7.**

Tablet ownership was 40% in 2012 and 25% in 2018.  
→ 用 **fell from A to B**（禁止 run-on）。
Tablet ownership fell from 40% in 2012 to 25% in 2018.  
### **8.**

Bus usage decreased from 55% in 2000 to 33% in 2010.  
→ 用 fell from A to B 造句。
Bus usage fell from 55% to 33%, between 2000 to in 2010.  
### **9.**

Paper book sales dropped from 1.2 million to 0.7 million between 2010 and 2020.
Paper book sales decreased by 0.5 million , falling from 1.2 million in 2010 to 0.7 million in 2020.
Paper book sales fell from 1.2 million in 2010 to 0.7 million  in 2020.

### **10.**

Employment in manufacturing decreased from 32% to 18% between 1990 and 2010.
Employment in manufacturing fell from 32%  in 1990 to 18% in 2010. 
Employment in manufacturing decreased by 14%, falling from 32%  in 1990 to 18% in 2010. 
### **11.**

Desktop computer ownership was 70% in 2005 and 45% in 2020.
Desktop computer ownership fell from 70% in 2005 to 45% in 2020.
Desktop computer ownership decreased by 25%, falling from 70% in 2005 to 45% in 2020.
### **12.**

DVD rentals fell from 50 million to 10 million units between 2008 and 2018.

DVD rentals decreased by 50 million, falling from 50 million to 10 million units between 2008 and 2018, which declined exactly a half .
### **13.**

Landline usage decreased from 85% to 40% between 2000 and 2020.
Landline usage decreased from 85% to 40% between 2000 and 2020.
