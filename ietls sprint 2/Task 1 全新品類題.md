# **② 流程題（Process）— 未練過類型**

**題目：**  
The diagram below shows how **bottled orange juice** is produced.  
Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
___
How **bottled orange juice** is produced is presented in the diagrams.

First of all, fresh oranges will be delivered by lorry, and then be washed.Following that, the washed oranges will then be sorted by their sizes , followed by being squeezed to extract juice. After that, they will be filtered  to remove seeds and pulp.And then, they will be **pasteurised** at high temperature.Finally, the last step of producing  **bottled orange juice** is to make the full processed juice be **bottled**, sealed, and labelled.

Once all these steps are done, bottled orange juice will be **packed** into boxes and delivered to supermarkets.
