特別注意：**research / advice / information / education / trade / production / pollution / data**。

      The government released many information regarding the new tax policy.

### B-2 改寫成自然 Task 1 句子（每題 1 句）

7. In 1990, the number of cars was 2 million. In 2000, it was 4 million.  
    → 用 increase / rise from A to B 合併成一句。
    The number of cars had doubled during this decade, from 2 million in 1990 to 4 million in 2000.
8. The percentage was 25% in Country A and 50% in Country B.（要強調 B 明顯較高）
     The percentage of Country A was half that of  Country B, 25% and 50% respectively.
9. Sales were 10,000 units in Q1, 10,500 in Q2, and 11,000 in Q3.  
    → 用「slight / gradual」描述穩定小幅上升。
	     Sales grew slightly from 10 thound units in Q1 to 11 thousand units in Q3.
10. The figure was 60% at the beginning, 40% in the middle, and 20% at the end.  
    → 用「steady decline」描述趨勢，不必逐點解釋。
    The figure decreased steadily  in this period, from 60% to 20%.
11. The production of coal dropped from 80 to 50 million tonnes, while the production of gas rose from 20 to 60 million tonnes.  
    → 合併成一句，強調「相反趨勢」。
    The production of coal dropped while the production of gas presented a reversed trend.  
12. The proportion of people using public transport did not change very much, staying between 30% and 35% throughout the period.  
    → 改寫成另一個自然版本。
     The proportion of people using public transport remianed stable,staying between 30% and 35% throughout the period.

## 🔹Part C – 句界＋連接詞（Run-on & Logic）10 題

任務：每題改寫成「一到兩句自然英文」，避免逗號亂接。  
連接詞盡量用：**and / but / so / although / while**。  
有因果再用 **because / so**。

1. Many people support strict punishments, others fear that this approach is too harsh.
    Many people support strict punishments, while others fear that this approach is too harsh.
2. The government increased spending on education, the quality of teaching did not improve.
    The government increased spending on education, whereas quality of teaching did not improve.
    The government increased spending on education, but quality of teaching did not improve.
    The government increased spending on education.However, quality of teaching did not improve.
3. Some adults return to school to learn new skills, this can help them stay competitive in the job market.
    Some adults return to school to learn new skills, for this can help them stay competitive in the job market.
    Some adults return to school to learn new skills,since for this can help them stay competitive in the job market.
    Some adults return to school to learn new skills, because it can help them stay competitive in the job market.
4. Online shopping is very convenient, it may also encourage people to buy unnecessary items.
     Online shopping is very convenient, but it may also encourage people to buy unnecessary items.
     Online shopping is very convenient, while it may also encourage people to buy unnecessary items.
     Online shopping is very convenient.However, it may also encourage people to buy unnecessary items.
5. The number of car accidents dropped significantly, people still feel unsafe on the roads.
    The number of car accidents dropped significantly, thus people still feel unsafe on the roads.
    The number of car accidents dropped significantly,therefore people still feel unsafe on the roads.
6. Public transport is cheap and efficient, many commuters still prefer to drive their own cars.
    Public transport is cheap and efficient.However, many commuters still prefer to drive their own cars.
    Public transport is cheap and efficient,  but many commuters still prefer to drive their own cars.
    Though public transport is cheap and efficient, many commuters still prefer to drive their own cars.
7. Some people argue that robots will create more jobs, others believe they will replace human workers.
    Some people argue that robots will create more jobs, while others believe they will replace human workers.
8. The city introduced new recycling policies, the amount of waste going to landfills decreased.
    The city introduced new recycling policies, causing the amount of waste going to landfills decreased.
    The city introduced new recycling policies,therefore the amount of waste going to landfills decreased.
    The city introduced new recycling policies.As aresult,  the amoun of waste going to landfills decreased.
9. Children should be allowed to make mistakes, they can learn valuable lessons from these experiences.
    Children should be allowed to make mistakes, and by which they can learn valuable lessons from these experiences.
    Children should be allowed to make mistakes so they can learn valuable lessons from these experiences.
10. Many citizens do not trust the government, they think that officials only care about their own interests.
Many citizens do not trust the government,since they think that officials only care about their own interests.
Many citizens do not trust the government,because they think that officials only care about their own interests.
Many citizens do not trust the government,for they think that officials only care about their own interests.
