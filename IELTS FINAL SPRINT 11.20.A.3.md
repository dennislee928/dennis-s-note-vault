# 🔹**Many / Much / A lot of / Number / Amount 精準練習題（10 題）**

任務：將每個句子改寫成**正確且自然的英文**。  
請特別注意：

- 可數 → many / a number of
    
- 不可數 → much / a lot of / the amount of
    
- jobs / crimes → 可數
    
- research / advice / information / pollution / traffic → 不可數
    
- data → 形式複數 → the **data show**
    
- work / trade → 不可數
    
- experience → 多為不可數（有時可數，但考場建議用不可數）
    

---

## **題目開始（10 題）**

### **1. There are many researches showing that people sleep less today.**
Much research shows that people sleep less nowadays.

### **2. Much students find it difficult to concentrate during online classes.**
Many students find it difficult to concentrate during online courses.

### **3. The amount of jobs in the tech sector has increased rapidly.**
The number of jobs in the tech sector has increased sharply.

### **4. Many informations about the policy were released yesterday.**
Much information about the policy was released yesterday.

### **5. The number of traffics during rush hour is getting worse.**
The number of traffic during rush hour is getting worse.

### **6. Much companies are now investing in AI-related products.**
Many companies are now investing AI-related products.

### **7. A large amount of crimes were reported last year.**
A large number of crimes was reported last year.
### **8. Many advice were given to first-year students.**
Much device was given to first-year students.
### **9. The data shows a lot of improvements in customer satisfaction.**
The data show lots of improvement in customer satisfaction.
The data show impressive improvement in customer satisfaction.
### **10. Much experiences are needed to get promoted in this field.**
Much experience is required to get promoted in this field.
