# **Band 8 模板組 #2**

（你等下要抄寫並固定肌肉記憶的 4 句）

---

# **① Overview（全新素材：energy sources）**

**Overall, the use of renewable energy grew steadily across the period, while fossil fuels remained the dominant source despite a gradual decline.**

**→ 特點：**

- 對比：renewable vs fossil
    
- grew steadily / remained dominant
    
- band 8 官方考題風格
    

mine-Overall, renewable energy usage grew steadily throughout the period, while fossil fuels remained the dominant source despite a gradual decline.

---

# **② increase from A to B（全新素材：solar power adoption）**

**Solar power adoption rose from just 10% in 2010 to 45% in 2020.**

→ 裸句但結構完美。

mine-Solar power adoption climbed from just 10% in 2010 to 45% in 2020.

---

# **③ rising from A to B + highest figure（全新素材：urban population）**

**The proportion of people living in urban areas increased markedly, rising from 55% in 1990 to 80% in 2020, the highest figure recorded across all regions.**

→ 用 markedly / recorded across — 更正式、更高分。

mine-The proportion of individuals living in urban areas increased drastically , rising from 55% in 1990 to 80% in 2020, the highest figure among all regions.

---

# **④ X rose…, followed by Y（全新素材：transport usage）**

**Car usage rose significantly, increasing from 40% in 2000 to 70% in 2020, followed by a more moderate rise in train usage, which climbed from 25% to 40% over the same period.**

→ 完整、自然、典型 Band 8 句子。
mine-Car usage increased significantly, rising form 40% in 2000 to 70% in 2020, followed by a more gental incline in train usage, which climbed from 25% to 40% over the same period.


---

# 📌 這 4 句的用途

這四句可以分別應用在：

- 技術採用（smartphones, internet, laptops…）
    
- 能源（renewables）
    
- 人口（urban/rural）
    
- 交通（cars, buses, trains）
    
- 任何折線圖、柱狀圖、比較類圖表
