# **micro-drill（4 題**

這 4 題會直接幫你強化：

- Overview 模板
    
- rise/peak 動詞模板
    
- Internet access 搭配
    
- followed by 結構
    

### **請你改寫以下 4 句為「Band 7+ 自然英文」**

---

### **1. All the devices increased, and smartphones grew the most.**

→ 改成類似 Band 8 Overview

Overall, ownerships of all these devices increased, while ownership of smartphones grew most substantially.

---

### **2. Laptops were 20% in 2005. In 2020, they were 80%.**

→ 用：**rose from A to B**
Laptops rose from 20% in 2005 to 80% in 2020(可接, followed by a ... in ... , which ....)

---

### **3. Internet was 40% in 2005 and 90% in 2020.**

→ 用：**rising from A to B** + **the highest figure**
The useage of internet rose from  40% in 2005 to 90% in 2020, which was the highest figure of all the four technologies.

---

### **4. Smartphone increased a lot, laptop increased after that.**

→ 用：**X rose…, followed by…**
Smartphone usage increased substantially, rising from XX% in XXXX to XX% XXXX, followed by a steady increase in laptop usage, which climbed from XX% in XXXX to XX% XXXX.
___
### **① Overview**

---

### **② Laptop ownership**

**Laptop ownership rose from 20% in 2005 to 80% in 2020.**

Laptop ownership rose from 20% in 2005 to 80% in 2020.

---

### **③ Internet access**

**Internet access also increased substantially, rising from 40% in 2005 to 90% in 2020, the highest figure among the four technologies.**

Internet access also increased substantially, rising from 40% in 2005 to 90% in 2020, the highest figure among the four technologies.

---

### **④ Smartphone followed by laptops**

**Smartphone usage increased substantially, rising from 5% in 2005 to 85% in 2020, followed by a steady rise in laptop usage, which climbed from 20% to 80% over the same period.**

Smartphone usage increased largely, rising from just 5% in 2005 to 85% in 2020, followed by a steady rise in laptop usage, which climbed from 20% to 80% over the same period.
