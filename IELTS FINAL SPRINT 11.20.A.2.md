### 🔹Part B – Task 1 動詞模板應用（6 題）

請用「指定動詞＋介系詞」寫一句 Task 1 句子。  
每題只要 1 句，但要包含年份或時間點。

1. increase **from A to B**
    
    - 情境：某國的 internet usage 在 2000 年是 20%，在 2020 年是 80%。
        The useage of internet of Japan  dramatically increased from 20% in 2000 to 80% in 2020.
1. increase **by X**
    
    - 情境：大學入學率從 40% 上升到 55%。
         The percentage of university enrollment had increased by 15%.
1. peak **at**
    
    - 情境：手機使用率在 2015 年達到 90%，之後略降。
        The useage of mobile phone peaked at 90% in 2015, followed by a slight decrease.
1. remain **stable at around**
    
    - 情境：失業率在整個期間大約維持在 6–7%。
         The percentage of unemployment reamined stable at around 6% to 7% during the whole period.
1. reach **a low of**
    
    - 情境：出生率在 2010 年降到 1.0 的最低點。
         The percentage of birth reached  a low of 1.0 in 2010.
1. fall **from A to B** / **by X**
    
    - 情境：紙本書銷量從 100 萬本降到 60 萬本。
        Sales of physical books made of paper fell from  1 million to 0.6 million.
