### Part B. Task 1 數據 + 介系詞專練（8 題）

#### B-1 填空（選 by / to / at / of）

1. The percentage of households with internet access increased __by_ 20% between 2010 and 2020.
    
2. It rose from 30% _to __ 55% in just five years.
    
3. The figure peaked __to _ 80% in 2018 before falling slightly.
    
4. Unemployment reached a low of_ 3% at the end of the period.
    
5. Car ownership remained stable _by__ around 60% throughout the decade.
    

#### B-2 改寫成自然 Task 1 句子（每題 1 句）

6. In 2000, the percentage was 40%. In 2010, the percentage was 70%.  
    → 合併成一個 Task 1 句子，使用「increase」或「rise」。
    The percentage increased sharply in this decade,from 30%  to 70%.
7. The number was 50 in Country A and 100 in Country B.（要表達 Country B 明顯比較高）
     The number of Country A was only exactly a half of that of Country B.
8. Sales were 30 million in 2015, then 45 million in 2016, and finally 60 million in 2017.  
    → 合併成一句，描述「穩定上升趨勢」。
      Sales increased very steadily during these 3 years,from 30 million to 45 million in 2016, and ended up with 60 million in 2017

### Part C. 句界 / 子句連接（Run-on 修正）（6 題）

任務：每題請改寫成**一到兩句自然英文**，避免 run-on，連接詞盡量簡單安全（although, while, because, so, and）。

1. Many people think fixed punishments are fair, others argue that judges need more flexibility.
    Many people think fixed punishments are fair, while others argue that judges need more flexibility.
    Many people think fixed punishments are fair, whereas others argue that judges need more flexibility.

2. Governments can monitor food supply chains, they can also educate citizens about healthy diets.
     Governments can monitor food supply chains,therefore they can educate citizens about healthy diets.
3. Robots can help people save time, they may also cause unemployment problems.
    Robots can help people save time, because they may also cause unemployment problems.
     Since they may also cause unemployment problems,robots can help people save time
4. Adults need education to improve their reading skills, this will directly increase national productivity.
     Adults need education to improve their reading skills.As a result,national productivity will drastically increase.
     Adults need education to improve their reading skills, and this will directly increase national productivity.
5. The number of train delays decreased sharply, passengers still complained about the service quality.
    The number of train delays decreased sharply, whereas passengers still complained about the service quality.
     The number of train delays decreased sharply, however, passengers still complained about the service quality.
     The number of train delays decreased sharply, while passengers still complained about the service quality.
6. Youths are sometimes forced to do unpaid community service, this might make them hate volunteering in the future.
    Youths are sometimes forced to do unpaid community service, and this might make them hate volunteering in the future.
    Youths are sometimes forced to do unpaid community service, causing them hate volunteering in the future.
