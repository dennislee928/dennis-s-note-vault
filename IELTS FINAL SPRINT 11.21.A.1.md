# **① OVERVIEW（最高 ROI 問題）**

你的版本：

> In general, all percentages increased with varying degree, while smartphone ownership showed the most significant growth. In addition, tablet didn't exist in early stage and its usages were the lowest among these four types of technology throughout the given period.

## ❌ 問題逐點（全都會扣分）

1. **with varying degree → 錯誤搭配**  
    正確是 **to varying degrees**。
    
2. **tablet didn’t exist → 語氣不自然 + 時態錯**  
    可改成 **tablets were the least common**  
    Task 1 不需要提「不存在」。
    
3. **its usages → usage（不可數）**  
    這是你今天最大錯誤之一：usage 不能加 s。
    
4. **Overview 不該放太細節（如 early stage、具體 explain）**  
    這會被扣 Cohesion。
___
In general, all percentages increased to varying degrees, while smartphone ownership showed the most significant growth. By contrast, tablets were the least popular ones.


___
# **② Fragment 修正句（Followed by）**

你的版本：

> Smartphone ownership increased most sharply, from only 5% in 2005 to 85% in 2020, followed by a steady growth of laptop ownership, which climbed from 20% 5 to 80% in the same period.

## ❌ 問題逐點

1. **steady growth of laptop ownership → 不自然搭配**  
    正確：**a steady rise in laptop ownership**
    
2. **climbed from 20% 5 to 80% → you have a typo（20% 5）**  
    這種 typo 在 Task 1 會被扣 Lexical accuracy。
    
3. “increased most sharply” 是好句，但可以更自然。
   
   ___

Smartphone ownership rose most sharply, increasing from only 5% in 2005 to 85% in 2020, followed by a steady rise in laptop ownership, which climbed from 20%  to 80% in the same period.

# **③ Internet 那句（邏輯散亂 + 不自然）**

你的版本：

> Meanwhile, the percentage of access to the Internet increased slightly by 50% during the same period. Moreover, it peaked at 90% in 2020, which was the highest figure shown in the table.

## ❌ 問題逐點

1. **percentage of access → 不自然（錯搭）**  
    正確：**Internet access**
    
2. **increased slightly by 50% → 語意矛盾**
    
    - “slightly” = 小幅
        
    - “by 50%” = 大幅  
        → 不可並用。
        
3. “Meanwhile” 在 Task 1 容易造成邏輯錯
    
4. “shown in the table” → 不自然（而且本來就是 line graph）-> 就請你忽略了
   ____ 
___
 Meanwhile, access to the Internet increased by 50%, strating from 40% in 2005 and peaked at 90% in 2020, which was the  all-time highest figure among all the 4  types of technology.
